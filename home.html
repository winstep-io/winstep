How it works

Three simple steps from sign-up to your first shot at the grand prize.

Play daily

Open three boxes a day, each with a short skill-based quiz. Unlock quick bonus games — Bridge, Spin Wheel, Hi-Lo — between them.

Earn points

Every correct answer and game adds to your round score. Each round, the top 10 earn championship points that carry across the season.

Win prizes

Finish on top of the championship when the season ends and claim the grand prize, awarded as Amazon.com Gift Cards.

Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before you start playing.

Is winstep really free?
Yes. Creating an account and playing is completely free, and no purchase or payment is ever required to enter or win. There are no paid offers to complete — you earn everything through the free daily quizzes and bonus games.
How do I earn points?
You earn round points by answering the daily quizzes inside your boxes, and by playing the bonus games (Bridge, Spin Wheel, and Hi-Lo) when they unlock. The more accurately and consistently you play, the higher you climb.
What are "boxes"?
Boxes are your daily challenges. Three unlock each day, each containing a quick skill-based quiz. They open one at a time, with a short break in between, and reset every day so there's always something new to play.
What are the bonus games?
Bridge, Spin Wheel, and Hi-Lo are quick mini-games that can unlock during the break after a box — if you've answered enough quiz questions correctly. They're worth bonus points but are only playable during that break.
How do rounds and championships work?
A round runs for a set period. When it ends, the top 10 players earn championship points on a motorsport-style scale. Those points add up across all the rounds in a season, and the player on top at the end wins the grand prize.
What prizes can I win?
Prizes are awarded as Amazon.com Gift Cards. The number of prizes and their value vary between championships and are shown on each championship's page before the drawing opens. Full details are in our Official Rules.
Is winstep affiliated with Amazon?
No. winstep is an independent platform. It is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Amazon, and Amazon is not a sponsor of any winstep promotion. Amazon.com Gift Cards are used only as prizes, subject to Amazon's own terms.
Who can take part?
You must be at least 18 years old and registered with winstep. The competition is void where prohibited or restricted by law, and some regions may be excluded from prize eligibility for certain promotions. See the Official Rules for details.
Do I have to buy anything or complete paid offers?
No. There are no paid tasks or offers. winstep is supported by standard on-page advertising, and you earn points purely by playing the free quizzes and games. No paid action will ever increase your chances of winning.
How are ties broken?
If two players finish level, the tie is broken in order by championship points, then total round points, then total activity, then number of correct quiz answers, and finally by who reached their total first. The full method is set out in the Official Rules.
Can I have more than one account?
No. Each player may hold only one account. Creating multiple or fake accounts, using bots, or colluding with others leads to disqualification and account termination. We verify gameplay on our servers to keep things fair.
How will I receive my prize?
When a championship ends, winners receive their prize by email as an Amazon.com Gift Card code — there's nothing extra to claim, since your age and eligibility are confirmed when you register. The one exception: U.S. winners who reach $600 or more in a calendar year will need to provide tax documentation before a prize is issued.
I forgot my password — what do I do?
On the log in page, enter your email and tap "Forgot password". We'll email you a secure link to set a new one. For your security, we can't see or change your password for you — recovery is always by email.
How is my data handled, and can I delete my account?
We explain exactly what we collect and why in our Privacy Policy. You can delete your account and personal data at any time from your profile page, or by emailing us.
