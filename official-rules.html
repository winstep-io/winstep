4. Winner determination & tie-breaker

At the conclusion of the Promotion Period, the leaderboard locks. Winners are determined by their final standings as recorded by the Sponsor's servers.

Tie-breaker: Final standings are ranked first by championship points. If two or more users are tied, the tie is broken in the following order: (1) higher championship points; (2) higher total round points; (3) higher total activity; (4) higher number of correct quiz answers; and finally (5) chronological priority — the user who reached that total first (based on the server timestamp recorded in our database) is ranked higher.
