🎁 Extra Round — how it works

When a championship ends, a 24-hour Extra Round opens — a bonus game called Box Rush, separate from the championship and its prizes.

Who can play: players who did not win a championship prize and reached the required activity (🎯) during the championship.

How Box Rush works:

  • 30 crates hide the shuffled numbers 1–30.
  • Smash any 10 crates — your score is the sum of what's inside.
  • After your 10th pick, every crate opens so you can see all the values, then your game is over.
  • One attempt per player — choose carefully!

The Extra Round Standings rank players by Box Rush score first, then ⭐ round points, 🎯 activity and ✅ correct answers. Extra Round prizes are paid to the top finishers.
