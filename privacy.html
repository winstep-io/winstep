Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how winstep ("winstep", "we", "us"), operated from the Republic of Georgia, collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use winstep.io (the "Service"). By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

winstep operates winstep.io, a free skill-and-chance competition platform where users complete tasks and play games to earn points and compete for prizes. The operator is based in the Republic of Georgia. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at team@winstep.io.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and chosen username. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see your plain password.
  • Google sign-in data — if you sign in with Google, we receive your basic profile information (email, and name where provided) to create and identify your account.
  • Activity data — your gameplay, answers, points, championship standings, and related progress, which are necessary to run the competition.
  • Technical and device data — IP address, device and browser type, and similar identifiers, collected through cookies, analytics, and our advertising partners.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To operate the Service, calculate points, run rounds and championships, and award prizes.
  • To communicate with you (e.g., email confirmation, account or prize notifications).
  • To measure and improve the Service using analytics.
  • To prevent fraud, cheating, and abuse, and to enforce our Terms.
  • To display advertising on the Service and measure its performance.

5. Third parties we share data with

We do not sell your personal data for money. We share limited data with service providers who help us operate the Service, each under their own privacy terms:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and hosting of your account data.
  • Vercel — website hosting and delivery.
  • Google — sign-in (OAuth) and analytics (Google Analytics).
  • Brevo — sending transactional emails (e.g., email confirmation).
  • Advertising partners — to display and measure the ads shown on the Service. These partners may set their own cookies and collect information such as your IP address and device/browser data to serve and measure ads, subject to their own privacy policies. Where we use Google's advertising products, you can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, our users, or the Service.

6. Legal bases (EU/EEA & UK users)

Where the GDPR applies, we process your data on the following bases: performance of our contract with you (to provide the Service), your consent (for non-essential cookies, advertising, and marketing), our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, and improving the Service), and compliance with legal obligations.

7. International data transfers

The operator is located in the Republic of Georgia, while our service providers (such as Supabase and Vercel) and our users may be located in the United States, the European Union, and elsewhere. This means your data may be transferred across borders. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent mechanisms.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. EU/EEA/UK users may also lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, use the controls in your profile or email team@winstep.io.

9. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. Because we use advertising partners that may track users across sites, our use of advertising cookies may be considered "sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising" under California law. California residents may opt out using the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link in our site footer, or by emailing team@winstep.io. We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and associated personal data at any time from your profile page, or by emailing team@winstep.io. Deletion is permanent and removes your account, points, and activity, except where we must retain limited information to comply with legal obligations or prevent abuse.

11. Data retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service and run the competition. After account deletion, we remove or anonymize your data within a reasonable period, except where retention is legally required.

12. Children

The Service is intended for users aged 18 and older and is a general-audience service that is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, and in particular not from children under 13 (in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, "COPPA") or below the applicable age of digital consent in the EU (GDPR, 13–16 depending on the country). If we become aware that we have collected data from a child, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact team@winstep.io.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections and access controls. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

15. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email team@winstep.io.
